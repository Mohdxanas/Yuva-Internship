Build it on Saturday. Break it. Build it better.

Workbench is a student makerspace with soldering irons, 3D printers and people who have already made the mistake you are about to make.

Join build night See what we made

What you can use

Electronics bench

Soldering stations, oscilloscopes and a wall of Arduino and ESP32 boards you can borrow.

Print and cut

Two 3D printers and a laser cutter. Book a slot, bring a file, leave with a part.

Mentors on call

Final-year students hold office hours every evening and review your wiring before you power it on.

Recent projects

Rooftop weather station

Four second-year students built a station that logs temperature, humidity and air quality every ten minutes and publishes the numbers to a simple dashboard. The first enclosure leaked in the monsoon, so the second one has a drip edge and a vented lid.
